Character art credits

Project Kingdom's male and female player and resident bodies, faces, hair, underwear, shirts, trousers, and boots use selected art from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator. The original art was available under CC BY-SA 3.0. The Kingdom sprites crop and recolor selected layers and are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Artists credited for the selected files: Benjamin K. Smith (BenCreating), bluecarrot16, Durrani, Eliza Wyatt (ElizaWy), Evert, JaidynReiman, Joe White, Johannes Sjölund (wulax), Manuel Riecke (MrBeast), Marcel van de Steeg (MadMarcel), Matthew Krohn (makrohn), MuffinElZangano, Nila122, Pierre Vigier (pvigier), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), and TheraHedwig.

Full credits by source file, original links, and licenses (CSV)

Kingdom changes: the original 64×64 walk frames were cropped to the complete 32×52 character span and normalized to a 32×48 frame. Selected poses were reordered to the game's down, left, right, up grid. Player trousers and boots were recolored, hair was changed to brown, and the trouser waist was recolored and shortened into linen briefs for the underwear preview. Resident clothing and hair were recolored by profession. The body, face, underwear, clothes, and hair remain separate image layers.

Hearth & Hound tavern art

The inn's counter, tables, stools, fireplace, cask, bread, mugs, candles, and entrance sconces use selected sprites from [LPC] Tavern by bluecarrot16 and collaborators, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. The original sprite sheets are included in assets/; they are cropped and scaled at runtime. See the full artist credits and source readme.

The inn's thatched roof and timber walls use selected sprites from [LPC] Thatched-roof Cottage, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. The original sheets are preserved as inn-thatch-roof.png and inn-cottage-wall.png; see the full attribution.

The inn's handcart, trough, log pile, and hanging sign use selected sprites from [LPC] Medieval Village Decorations, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. The original sheet is inn-yard-decorations.png; see the full artist credits.

The inn's windows, door, and chimney use selected tiles from Medieval Tileset by Calciumtrice, licensed under CC BY 3.0. The source sheet is included as inn-medieval-exterior.png.

The inn's wood floor and interior back wall use tiles from the same set, preserved as inn-medieval-interior.png.

The upstairs guest beds are cropped from [LPC] House Insides by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. The complete bed crop is preserved as inn-guest-bed.png.
