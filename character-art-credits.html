Character art credits

Project Kingdom's male and female player bodies, faces, hair, underwear, shirts, trousers, and boots use selected art from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator. The original art was available under CC BY-SA 3.0. The Kingdom sprites crop and recolor selected layers and are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Artists credited for the selected files: Benjamin K. Smith (BenCreating), bluecarrot16, Durrani, Eliza Wyatt (ElizaWy), Evert, JaidynReiman, Joe White, Johannes Sjölund (wulax), Manuel Riecke (MrBeast), Marcel van de Steeg (MadMarcel), Matthew Krohn (makrohn), MuffinElZangano, Nila122, Pierre Vigier (pvigier), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), and TheraHedwig.

Full credits by source file, original links, and licenses (CSV)

Kingdom changes: the original 64×64 walk frames were cropped to the complete 32×52 character span and normalized to a 32×48 frame. Selected poses were reordered to the game's down, left, right, up grid. Trousers and boots were recolored, hair was changed to brown, and the trouser waist was recolored and shortened into linen briefs for the underwear preview. The body, face, underwear, clothes, and hair remain separate image layers.
